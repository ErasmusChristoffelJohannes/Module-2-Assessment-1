Iced Coffee

Prep Time: 5 minutes

Yield: 2

Ingredients






Steps

  1. Brew coffee and allow it to cool to room temperature.
  2. Fill two tall glasses with ice cubes.
  3. Pour cooled coffee over the ice.
  4. Add milk (or cream) and sugar; stir well.
  5. Top with whipped cream and drizzle with chocolate or caramel if desired.
  6. Serve immediately with a straw.
