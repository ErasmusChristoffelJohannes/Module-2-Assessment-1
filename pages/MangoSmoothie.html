Mango Smoothie

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Yield: 2

Ingredients





Steps

  1. Place mango chunks, yogurt, milk (or juice), and honey into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Add ice cubes if desired and blend again until frothy.
  4. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.
  5. Pour into glasses and serve immediately, garnished with a mango slice or mint leaf.
