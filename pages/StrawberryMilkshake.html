Strawberry Milkshake

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Yield: 2

Ingredients





Steps

  1. Place strawberries, ice cream, milk, and sugar (if using) into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.
  4. Pour into chilled glasses.
  5. Top with whipped cream and a fresh strawberry garnish if desired.
