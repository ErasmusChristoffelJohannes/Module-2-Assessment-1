Caesar Salad

Prep Time: 15 minutes

Yield: 4

Ingredients







Steps

  1. Wash and chop romaine lettuce; place in a large salad bowl.
  2. Add croutons and Parmesan cheese.
  3. In a small bowl, mix Caesar dressing with garlic and lemon juice.
  4. Pour dressing over salad; toss well to coat evenly.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, then serve immediately.
