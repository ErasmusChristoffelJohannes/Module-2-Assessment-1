Chocolate Milkshake

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Yield: 2

Ingredients




Steps

  1. Place chocolate ice cream, milk, and chocolate syrup into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Taste and adjust sweetness or chocolate flavor if desired.
  4. Pour into chilled glasses.
  5. Top with whipped cream and garnish with chocolate shavings or sprinkles.
