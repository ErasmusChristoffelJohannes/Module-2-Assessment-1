Double Chocolate Cookies

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Yield: 24 cookies

Ingredients










Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, cream butter, granulated sugar, and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  3. Beat in egg and vanilla extract.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, cocoa powder, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Gradually add dry ingredients to wet mixture; stir until combined.
  6. Fold in chocolate chips.
  7. Drop spoonfuls of dough onto prepared baking sheet, spacing evenly.
  8. Bake for 10–12 minutes, or until edges are set and centers look slightly soft.
  9. Cool on a wire rack before serving.
