Classic Beef Burger

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Yield: 4

Ingredients









Steps

  1. Preheat grill or skillet to medium-high heat.
  2. Season ground beef with salt and pepper; form into 4 equal patties.
  3. Cook patties for 4–5 minutes per side, or until desired doneness.
  4. Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty during the last minute of cooking; cover to melt.
  5. Toast buns lightly on the grill or skillet.
  6. Assemble burgers: place lettuce, tomato, and onion on the bottom bun, top with the patty and cheese, add condiments, then finish with the top bun.
  7. Serve immediately with fries or salad.
