Vanilla Milkshake

Prep Time: 10 minutes

Yield: 2

Ingredients





Steps

  1. Place vanilla ice cream, milk, sugar (if using), and vanilla extract into a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth and creamy.
  3. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.
  4. Pour into chilled glasses.
  5. Top with whipped cream and a drizzle of caramel or chocolate syrup if desired.
